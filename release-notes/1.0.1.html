Touch works

  • Taps on the touchscreen MacBook Pro now play. macOS 27 delivers finger touches to apps only through gesture recognizers, and 1.0 listened the old way, so touches never arrived. Chords and glissandos work as designed.
  • The Apple logo pulse is now a quick blink: the logo goes dark the instant a note is struck and comes back a moment later, ready for the next one. Turning Music Touch on no longer keeps the logo dark.
  • The piano now follows the Mac's default audio output and switches with it, so it plays through whatever is selected in Sound settings: speakers, headphones, AirPods or a display.
  • Settings › Status shows the audio output in use and what the last input was (touch or click), to help check that the screen is being heard.
  • Music Touch now updates itself. Check for Updates… is in the menu bar menu, and automatic checks can be turned off in Settings.
