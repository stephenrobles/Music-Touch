Every zone, and a tunable blink

  • Notes are now placed from the touch's position on the screen surface rather than its reported view location, so every zone plays, not just the top one.
  • Settings › Apple Logo has a Blink length slider, from 0.05 to 1.5 seconds, so the dark interval can be tuned to how fast the logo itself fades.
  • Settings › Status shows the raw coordinates of the last touch, to help diagnose mapping on real hardware.
