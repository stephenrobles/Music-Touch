Any key, any size, and touches that work

  • Choose a key in Settings › Key: any root from C to B, major or natural minor. The zones, their labels and the notes they play follow.
  • Choose how many zones share the screen: one octave (8 notes) up to 12. The screen divides evenly and the colours spread across the same warm-to-cool run.
  • On the MacBook Pro with Touchscreen, taps now play every zone. Reading a touchscreen touch's trackpad-only position raised an internal AppKit error that silently swallowed the tap; the app no longer reads it.
  • Every zone makes a sound. The nine samplers were all wired to a filter with a single input, so only the top one was connected; a mixer now sums them.
  • A trackpad click on the overlay no longer stops screen touches.
  • The Apple logo pulse is the right way round: it rests dark while Music Touch is on, lights the instant a note is struck and goes dark as the note starts to fade. macOS offers no dimming for the logo, so it switches rather than fades; the Blink length slider is gone.
  • The Settings window has a fixed width and can be resized vertically.
